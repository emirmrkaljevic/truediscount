# Privacy policy

truediscount does not collect, transmit or sell any data.

- Product name, product URL path, price and date are stored locally in your browser so the extension can compare prices over time.
- No data leaves your device. The extension makes no network requests.
- You can delete all stored data from the extension popup ("Clear all data") or by removing the extension.

The use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
