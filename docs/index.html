Black Friday fake discounts in the EU

Every November, "-50%" appears on products that cost the same a few weeks earlier. Since 2022, EU law says the "old price" of a sale must be the lowest price of the previous 30 days (Directive 2019/2161, Article 6a of the Price Indication Directive). Consumer authorities still find shops that inflate the reference price. truediscount lets you check it yourself, right on the product page.

What it does

🟢 Real discountThe old price matches the lowest price of the last 30 days.
🔴 Inflated discountThe product was cheaper recently, so the advertised percentage is overstated.
📉 Price graphA mini chart of every price you have seen for the product.
🔔 Price alertsSet a target price and get a notification when a visit records a price at or below it.
📋 WatchlistAll tracked products in the popup with latest price, 30-day low and drop from the 30-day high. Sort by biggest drop.
💾 Backup, restore & CSVExport your price history to JSON (for backup and restore) or CSV (for Excel and Sheets). Merged per day, nothing uploaded.
🙈 Hide on any siteTurn the badge off for a domain from the popup and manage the list on the settings page. Nothing is recorded there.
⚡ Works on first visitReads the shop's own "lowest price in 30 days" label when it is shown.
🏷️ RRP checkTells you when the crossed-out price is only the manufacturer's recommended price.
🔒 PrivateEverything stays in your browser. No account, no tracking, no network requests.

Install in 60 seconds

truediscount is not in the browser stores yet, so you load it yourself:

Chrome, Edge, Brave
  1. Download the Chrome zip from the latest release and unzip it.
  2. Open chrome://extensions (or edge://extensions, brave://extensions).
  3. Turn on Developer mode.
  4. Click Load unpacked and pick the folder.
Firefox
  1. Download the Firefox zip from the latest release.
  2. Open about:debugging#/runtime/this-firefox.
  3. Click Load Temporary Add-on and pick the zip.
Temporary add-ons are removed on restart. A signed version on addons.mozilla.org is on the way.

Full steps, including how to update and how to keep it installed in Firefox Developer Edition, are in the README.

FAQ

How do I know if a Black Friday discount is fake?

Compare the crossed-out price with the lowest price of the last 30 days. If the product was cheaper in that period, the discount is inflated. truediscount does this comparison automatically.

What is the EU Omnibus 30-day rule?

Directive (EU) 2019/2161 requires that any announced price reduction shows the prior price, defined as the lowest price the trader applied in at least the 30 days before the reduction.

Is the verdict legal advice?

No. It is information based on prices seen in your browser and on what the shop discloses. To report a shop, contact your national consumer authority.

No. See the privacy policy.
