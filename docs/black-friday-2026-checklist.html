Black Friday 2026 checklist

Black Friday is on 27 November 2026. Five steps to tell a real deal from a dressed-up one.

1. Start tracking now, not on the day

A discount is only meaningful compared with earlier prices. Visit the products on your wishlist a few times in November. With truediscount installed, every visit stores one price per day in your browser, so by Black Friday you have your own price history.

2. Compare the old price with the 30-day low

In the EU, a price reduction must be announced against the lowest price of the previous 30 days (Directive 2019/2161, see the 30-day rule by country). If the item was cheaper three weeks ago, a "-40%" next to a higher old price is overstated. Many shops print their own 30-day low next to the price; truediscount reads it on the first visit.

3. Watch out for RRP and UVP comparisons

A crossed-out "RRP", "UVP" or "manufacturer's price" is not an earlier price of the shop. It is allowed, but the percentage often looks bigger than any real saving. truediscount labels these separately.

4. Check your whole wishlist in one place

Open the popup and switch the Watchlist to Biggest drop. It lists every product you visited with the latest price, the 30-day low and how far it is below its 30-day high. Prices update when you open a product page; the extension does not load shop pages in the background.

5. Set a target price and stop refreshing

Click Alert me below on the badge and pick your price. When a visit records a price at or below it, you get a browser notification and the product is marked in the watchlist.

Quick list

  • Install the extension before mid November.
  • Visit each wishlist product at least once a week.
  • On the day: is the price below the 30-day low, or only below an inflated old price?
  • Is the crossed-out price an RRP rather than the shop's own earlier price?
  • Delivery costs and return conditions still count.
