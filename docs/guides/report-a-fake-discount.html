If a shop shows a discount that is not calculated from its lowest price in the last 30 days, you can report it. National consumer authorities enforce the rule; see real enforcement cases. If the shop is in another EU country, the European Consumer Centre (ECC) in your own country can help for free.

Before you report

  1. Take a screenshot showing the sale price, the crossed-out price, the 30-day lowest price line (or that it is missing), the date and the page address.
  2. If you have them, add earlier screenshots or a price history showing the lower price.
  3. Note the shop name and the country it sells from.

FAQ

Where do I report a fake discount in the EU?

Report it to your national consumer authority, for example SignalConso in France, ConsuWijzer (ACM) in the Netherlands or UOKiK's consumer portal in Poland. If the shop is in another EU country, contact the European Consumer Centre in your own country.

What evidence should I keep?

A dated screenshot of the product page showing the sale price, the crossed-out price and the 30-day lowest price line, plus any earlier screenshots or price history showing a lower price.

Does reporting cost anything?

No. Reporting to a national consumer authority or getting help from a European Consumer Centre is free.
