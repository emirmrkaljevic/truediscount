# Fake discount fines in the EU

Court rulings and fines over fake old prices under the 30-day lowest price rule, each with its official source. Back to the [guides index](README.md) or the [README](../../README.md). Web version: [enforcement cases](https://emirmrkaljevic.github.io/truediscount/guides/omnibus-enforcement-cases.html).

## What these cases have in common

- The crossed-out price must be a price the shop really charged, and the lowest one in the last 30 days.
- The percentage must be calculated from that price, not from a list price or the price the day before.
- Authorities look at the whole site, including category pages and ads.
