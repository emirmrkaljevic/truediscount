# How to check if a Black Friday discount is real

A one-minute check for any EU product page, plus a checklist for Black Friday on 27 November 2026. Back to the [guides index](README.md) or the [README](../../README.md). Web version: [discount check](https://emirmrkaljevic.github.io/truediscount/black-friday-discount-check.html) and [checklist](https://emirmrkaljevic.github.io/truediscount/black-friday-2026-checklist.html).

## The rule

Since 28 May 2022, Article 6a of Directive 98/6/EC (added by the Omnibus Directive 2019/2161) says that when a shop announces a price reduction, the prior price must be the lowest price that shop charged in the 30 days before the reduction. In case C-330/23 (Aldi Süd, 2024) the EU Court of Justice confirmed that a percentage like "-30%" must also be calculated from that price.

Example: a TV cost 499 euro for most of October, was briefly raised to 699 euro and is now "499 euro, was 699 euro, -29%". The reference should be 499 euro, so the real discount is zero.

Countries may allow exceptions, for example for goods that spoil quickly or products on sale for less than 30 days.

## The five-step check

1. Find out what the crossed-out price is. "RRP", "UVP", "adviesprijs" or "retail price" means a manufacturer price, not the shop's own earlier price.
2. Look for the 30-day lowest price near the deal, for example "Lowest price in the last 30 days" or "Niedrigster Preis der letzten 30 Tage".
3. Recalculate: discount = (30-day lowest minus sale price) divided by 30-day lowest. If that is far below the advertised percentage, the deal is inflated.
4. Check the history. Visit the products you want in the weeks before, so you know the normal price.
5. Report clear cases to your national consumer authority. See [where to report a fake discount](report-a-fake-discount.md).

## Black Friday checklist

- Install truediscount before mid November and visit each wishlist product at least once a week. Every visit stores one price per day in your browser.
- On the day, ask whether the price is below the 30-day low or only below an inflated old price.
- Check whether the crossed-out price is an RRP rather than the shop's own earlier price.
- Use the popup watchlist sorted by **Biggest drop** to see your whole wishlist in one place, and set a target price with **Alert me below**.
- Delivery costs and return conditions still count.
