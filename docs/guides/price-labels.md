# 30-day price labels by country

How shops word the 30-day lowest price, and the manufacturer price labels that the rule does not cover. Back to the [guides index](README.md) or the [README](../../README.md). Web version: [the 30-day rule by country](https://emirmrkaljevic.github.io/truediscount/eu-30-day-lowest-price-rule.html).

Every EU country has written Article 6a of Directive 98/6/EC into national law (in Germany it is section 11 of the PAngV). This is typical wording, not an official list.

| Country | Language | 30-day lowest price label | Manufacturer price labels (not covered) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Germany, Austria | German | Niedrigster Preis der letzten 30 Tage; Gesamtpreis der letzten 30 Tage | UVP, Unverbindliche Preisempfehlung, statt |
| France, Belgium, Luxembourg | French | Prix le plus bas des 30 derniers jours; Prix de référence | PVC, Prix conseillé |
| Netherlands, Belgium | Dutch | Laagste prijs van de afgelopen 30 dagen | Adviesprijs, van |
| Italy | Italian | Prezzo più basso degli ultimi 30 giorni | Prezzo consigliato |
| Spain | Spanish | Precio más bajo de los últimos 30 días | PVPR, Precio recomendado |
| Portugal | Portuguese | Preço mais baixo dos últimos 30 dias | PVP recomendado |
| Poland | Polish | Najniższa cena z 30 dni przed obniżką | Cena katalogowa, Cena sugerowana |
| Czechia | Czech | Nejnižší cena za posledních 30 dní | Doporučená cena |
| Slovakia | Slovak | Najnižšia cena za posledných 30 dní | Odporúčaná cena |
| Hungary | Hungarian | Az elmúlt 30 nap legalacsonyabb ára | Ajánlott fogyasztói ár |
| Romania | Romanian | Cel mai mic preț din ultimele 30 de zile | PRP, Preț recomandat |
| Croatia, Slovenia | Croatian, Slovenian | Najniža cijena u posljednjih 30 dana; Najnižja cena v zadnjih 30 dneh | Preporučena cijena; Priporočena cena |
| Sweden | Swedish | Lägsta pris senaste 30 dagarna | Rekommenderat pris, Ord. pris |
| Denmark | Danish | Laveste pris de seneste 30 dage | Vejledende pris, Før |
| Finland | Finnish | Alin hinta viimeisen 30 päivän aikana | Suositushinta |

truediscount reads these labels on the first visit to a product page. See [the EU 30-day lowest price rule](../omnibus.md).
